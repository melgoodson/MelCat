Big Mel — Full Feature Guide

MelCat Storefront Companion + Admin Dashboard · Snarky Pets

Part 1 — Storefront Companion

The companion is a fully animated Big Mel mascot that lives in the bottom-right corner of the store. It engages shoppers with contextual snark and drives them toward conversion.

1. Context-Aware Messages

Mel responds to where the customer is on the site — he never says the same thing everywhere.

  • Welcome: On first visit, he walks in and greets: "Hey. Welcome to Snarky Pets. Try not to be boring."
  • Home / Collections: Judges indecision: "I can smell the indecision from here."
  • Product Page: Pressures them to buy: "That thing's been staring at you for 30 seconds."
  • Cart Page: Encourages checkout: "You're SO close. Don't chicken out now."

2. Call-to-Action Buttons (CTAs) Selective

Not every message has a button — only the ones where it makes sense to take action.

  • "Shop more chaos" — homepage, links to collections page.
  • "Add it already →" — product pages, auto-scrolls to the Add to Cart button.
  • "Checkout →" — cart page, goes straight to payment.

3. Add-to-Cart Celebration

Big Mel automatically detects when a product is added to cart and reacts with a jump animation and a sarcastic approval message like "Finally. A financially responsible bad decision."

4. Idle & Random Snark

If the user sits on a page for 45–90 seconds doing nothing, Mel drops a random unprompted comment to re-engage them — up to 3 times per session.

  • "Still here. Still judging."
  • "You've been on this page a while. Commitment or confusion?"
  • "This silence is comfortable. For me. Less so for your cart."

5. Life-like Animations (State Machine)

Big Mel uses high-quality, custom-animated GIFs hosted on the Shopify CDN for maximum performance.

StateWhat Happens
Walk-inFirst visit: Big Mel slides in from the bottom of the screen
IdleGentle breathing animation plays on loop
ProwlingSmoothly walks left/right (JS pacing matches 4s CSS linear transition to prevent gliding)
SleepingHeavy sleeping animation when user is completely idle
CelebrateJumps and glows when an item is added to cart
WatchingJudging animation when user scrolls 30%+ down the page

6. "Tap Me" Hint System

If a new visitor doesn't interact within 9 seconds, a subtle orange ring pulses around Big Mel and a tiny "tap me" label appears briefly. Once clicked, the hint disables itself permanently for that session.

7. Shopper-Friendly Rules

  • Session Dismiss: The "X" button hides Big Mel for the entire browsing session.
  • Checkout Blackout: Completely disabled on the checkout page — zero distractions during payment.
  • Bubble Pause: Hovering over a speech bubble pauses its auto-hide timer.
  • Performance: Built in pure vanilla JS/CSS — zero impact on store speed.
  • Accessibility: Fully disables all animations if the customer has "Reduce Motion" turned on in their OS.

8. Asset Management Architecture

  • Shopify CLI Limits: Extension assets/ folders cannot handle large .gif or .webp files.
  • Global CDN Hosting: Emotion GIFs (e.g. melcat-idle.gif) are uploaded directly to the Shopify Admin under Content > Files.
  • Liquid Integration: The companion.liquid block uses the file_url filter to dynamically pull these global assets into the JavaScript state machine.

Part 2 — MelCat Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard lives inside your Shopify app (MelCat Dashboard) and is the merchant-facing control centre for the entire Big Mel digital product ecosystem.

Home — Analytics Overview

The main page shows real-time performance stats, filterable by last 7 days, 30 days, or all-time.

MetricWhat It Tracks
Total CustomersTotal unique buyers in the system
Active EntitlementsCustomers currently with an active digital pack access
QR RedemptionsHow many times a physical-product QR code was scanned & claimed
Library ViewsTimes customers opened their digital vault/library
Asset DownloadsTotal file downloads across all packs
Upgrade ClicksCustomers who clicked an upgrade offer
Upgrade PurchasesCustomers who completed an upgrade purchase
Drop DownloadsDownloads from exclusive content drops

It also shows Funnel Conversion Rates: QR Claim → Vault, Vault → Upgrade Click, Click → Purchase, Drop Notification → Download.

Digital Packs

Packs are the "folders" that hold your digital content. Each pack belongs to a tier (Lite, Standard, Deluxe, or Ultimate).

  • Create, name, and assign packs to access tiers.
  • See how many assets and QR campaigns are linked to each pack.
  • Delete/deactivate packs when no longer needed.

Example: Create a pack called "Big Mel Sticker Pack Vol.1" and assign it to the Standard tier.

Assets & Storage

Upload and organise the actual digital files (GIFs, stickers, wallpapers, PDFs) that customers download from their library. Each asset is attached to a Pack so access is tier-gated automatically.

Content Drops

Schedule exclusive content releases for specific tiers. When a drop goes live, customers with the required tier get notified and can download the new content — driving retention and repeat engagement.

  • Set a drop title and required tier level.
  • Schedule a future release date.
  • Track notifications sent, failures, and download rates per drop.

Order Rules (Variant Mapping)

Links specific Shopify product variants to digital packs. When a customer purchases a mapped variant, they are automatically granted access to the correct digital pack — no manual steps needed.

Example: Buying the "Snarky Cat Mug — Deluxe Bundle" variant automatically unlocks the Deluxe pack in their library.

QR Campaigns

The physical-to-digital bridge. Generates unique QR codes to be printed on product inserts shipped inside physical orders.

  • Create a campaign and link it to a specific digital pack.
  • A customer scans the QR code from their box and claims their free digital pack.
  • Track redemptions, unique users, and claim completion rates per campaign.

Customer Database

A full view of every customer who has interacted with the digital system.

  • View each customer's email, active entitlements, and tier level.
  • See their full history: QR scans, downloads, and upgrade activity.
  • Useful for customer support and auditing access issues.
